Homemade Ice Cream in Port Jefferson

Our Flavors

We churn small-batch ice cream every morning. Try our vanilla bean, chocolate fudge, mint chip and seasonal gelato. Soft serve cones, waffle cones and dipped cones are always available.

Sundaes and Milkshakes

Build your own sundae with hot fudge, sprinkles and whipped cream, or order a thick milkshake. Dairy-free sorbet and vegan ice cream options too.

Ice Cream Cakes and Catering

Order custom ice cream cakes for birthdays and parties. Our ice cream truck caters events across Port Jefferson and Suffolk County.

Visit our ice cream shop at 12 Main Street, Port Jefferson, NY. Open daily noon to 10pm. Family owned since 1998.
