Why not just buy a list?

Buying a giant, generic lead list can look like the easy route. The data may be stale, the businesses may not fit your market, and you still have to figure out who is actually worth contacting.

LeadMeLeads takes a different approach: search by keyword and location, then review local businesses that match the search instead of starting with a recycled spreadsheet.

Primary tool

Lead Finder

Enter a keyword and a city, state, or ZIP code. LeadMeLeads searches for matching local businesses, then reviews what is publicly available about each one. You get a shortlist of business leads worth contacting, not a spreadsheet of strangers.

  • Search by keyword and city, state, or ZIP code
  • Review businesses and what is publicly available about them
  • See contact details when we find them, and why the lead may be worth pursuing

We check for emails, phone numbers, and addresses — but we do not always find every one, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Secondary tool

Website Comparison Tool

Pick two websites — yours and a competitor's, or two competitors — and see the practical differences: titles, meta descriptions, headings, and content gaps. Built for specific answers, not a vague score that means nothing outside a sales pitch.

  • Compare titles, meta descriptions, and headings side by side
  • Spot missing content and structural gaps
  • Get plain recommendations, not a mystery score

Useful information, not a scoreboard

We are not interested in vanity metrics or scary numbers designed to make you panic-buy an SEO package. LeadMeLeads shows you what is actually there: real contact details when we can find them, real website gaps when they exist, and a plain reason for every lead.

If we cannot find something, we say so instead of making it up.
