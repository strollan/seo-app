LeadMeLeads provides tools for local business research, website comparison, and related internal workflow utilities.

Information we may process

When you use LeadMeLeads, we may process information you provide directly, such as account details, search inputs, website URLs, and other information needed to provide the requested service.

LeadMeLeads may also collect publicly available business and website information as part of its research tools.

WhatsApp and Slayer Status

LeadMeLeads operates an internal utility called Slayer Status that may use the WhatsApp Business Platform to receive status requests and send workflow-status responses.

When this integration is used, Meta may provide message-related information such as the sender's WhatsApp identifier, message content, message timestamps, and delivery-status information.

This information is used only to process the requested status command, deliver the response, troubleshoot the integration, and maintain service reliability.

We do not sell WhatsApp message data or use it for advertising.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide requested features and services;
  • operate and maintain the application;
  • troubleshoot errors and improve reliability;
  • prevent abuse or misuse;
  • maintain account, workflow, and operational records where necessary.

Third-party services

LeadMeLeads may rely on third-party service providers to operate certain features. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The Slayer Status WhatsApp integration uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, operational requirements, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.
